🕌 Helpful halal tip

In China, look for the Chinese characters 清真 (qīngzhēn), commonly used to indicate halal food. Still ask staff to confirm ingredients and preparation.

“请问这是清真的吗？”

Qǐngwèn zhè shì qīngzhēn de ma? — Is this halal?
